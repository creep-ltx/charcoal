zx-spectrum-48-charset.font is bytes 0x3D00-0x3FFF (the character set, codes 32-127) of the ZX Spectrum
48K ROM (48.rom, SHA-1 5ea7c2b824672e914525d1d5c419d71b84a426a2) as distributed with the Fuse emulator.

From Fuse's roms/README.copyright: "These are copyright Amstrad, who allow distribution of the ROMs but
retain the copyright. You may not sell the ROMs or embed the ROMs in hardware, although it is allowed to
sell a product which contains the ROMs, so long as the charge is being made for the product, not for the
ROMs themselves."
