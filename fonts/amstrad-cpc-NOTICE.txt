amstrad-cpc-charset.font is bytes 0x3800-0x3FFF (the character set, codes 0-255, 8 bytes each) of the
Amstrad CPC operating system (firmware) ROM, as distributed with the Caprice32 emulator
(github.com/ColinPitrat/caprice32, rom/cpc6128.rom, SHA-1 5977adbad3f7c1e0e082cd02fe76a700d9860c30).
The CPC 464 (rom/cpc464.rom, 56d39c463da60968d93e58b4ba0e675829412a20) and 664 ROMs hold the same
2048 bytes (SHA-1 of the character set: 0718dc1d75187837154ba11eae1220ca53b81cdf).
