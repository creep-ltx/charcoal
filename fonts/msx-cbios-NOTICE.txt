msx-cbios.font, msx-cbios-jp.font, msx-cbios-br.font: the character sets of C-BIOS 0.29a
(cbios.sourceforge.net), a free MSX BIOS. msx-cbios.font is the international set
(src/font_us.asm, "Font designed by Eric Boon"), msx-cbios-jp.font the Japanese one
(src/font_jp.asm), msx-cbios-br.font the Brazilian one (src/font_br.asm); made by
tools/msxmz-fonts.py. They are not the fonts of Microsoft's MSX BIOS (which may not be
passed on): an MSX shows its own ROM's characters for a page in the C-BIOS font.
