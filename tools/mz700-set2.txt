# CharEdit's own look-alike of the Sharp MZ-700's second character set
# (display codes 0x00-0xFF with the attribute's bit 7 set). Drawn by hand for
# CharEdit, 2026-10-16, from what each character means (Unicode's names for
# them, mz.go's mzRunes) with the MZ-700 owner's character table as a picture
# of the look; nothing copied from Sharp's ROM. CC0 (fonts/mz700-set2-NOTICE.txt).
# tools/mz700-set2.py turns this into fonts/mz700-set2.font.
#
# Forms:
#   XX            then 8 rows of 8 ('#' ink, '.' paper)
#   skel XX       then 6 rows of 5: the letter's middle line; the glyph is the
#                 wall around it (the MZ's outlined letters and digits)
#   XX = op YY    op: cw ccw 180 flipx flipy (turned or mirrored copy of YY)
#   big A B C D   then 16 rows of 16: a picture of 2x2 characters (A upper
#                 left, B upper right, C lower left, D lower right; - none)
#   pie XX side   the large black circle less its quarter on side (up left down right)
